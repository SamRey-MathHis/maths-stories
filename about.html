What the project aims to do

Every story on this site:

  • starts from the historical problem that led to the discovery,
  • explains how people reasoned about it,
  • is told as an engaging narrative, and
  • connects the idea to the people and cultures who discovered it, across China, India, the Islamic world, Europe and beyond.
